## 5. The strategy deck (June 2026) in brief

**Format:** the 35-second question-led template. 0 to 3s question on screen and spoken. 3 to 30s answer demonstrated on the sauna with specs and one vivid metaphor. 30 to 40s "And don't forget..." kicker. 40 to 45s end card only.

**Positioning:** "Sauna for people who take their health seriously. Built by the only company that has been doing this since 1995, and the only one that also builds the salt caves that go beside them." Five gaps competitors leave open: trusted veteran advisor, salt cave and halotherapy integration, the serious and professional buyer, science-meets-tradition, 30-year insider sourcing authority.

**Competitors benchmarked:** Clearlight (2.6K subs, 2.4M top short, celebrity and luxury), HigherDOSE, Sunlighten, Almost Heaven, SaunaSpace, Plunge (200+ shorts, streak culture), Finnleo, Dynamic. Sun Home Saunas was added by Rick on Sep 10 as the one to learn from.

**Rick's voice guide.** Lean into: mid-action openers, "Now" as a pivot, "And don't forget" before the kicker, dimensions and weights from memory, name the beneficiary before the product, "we've been doing this since 1995," mechanism plus metaphor plus outcome. Avoid: flowery openers, hype energy, long subordinate clauses, more than 3 or 4 specs per breath, saying the phone number mid-sentence, corporate vocabulary, repeating "halogenerator," re-explaining negative ions every script.

**Rick's best line:** "Each molecule of salt that goes into the lungs acts like a magnet for toxins, and carries that out of the body through the digestive tract."

**28 short ideas** organized by unit (one-person 1 to 7, two-person outdoor 8 to 12, three-person outdoor 13 to 17, Mega four-person 18 to 23, cross-sauna authority 24 to 28) and **10 word-for-word scripts** are in the deck at `SpiritualQuest/JULY/RAW/spiritualquest_shorts_strategy.pdf` in Dropbox. Scripts 1 to 10 were all shot in July.

**Salt content pillar (Rick, Jun 30):** negative ions get their own dedicated 40-second short, salt plus oxygen is a content pillar not a one-off, bank close-up B-roll of Himalayan salt for a year of overlays. A second batch of 10 shorts purely on halotherapy is on the table.
