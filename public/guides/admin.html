03 — The core workflow

Add a shoot in one screen

Manually arranging a shoot used to mean four screens. Now it’s one form: client, property, photographer, and the upload link — all created together.

Open New shoot

Click + New shoot from anywhere, or Orders → New shoot.

Pick the client — or add one on the spot

Search an existing agent, or flip to New client and type their name + email. No need to pre-create anyone.

Type the property address

Start typing in Street Address and choose from the dropdown — city, state and ZIP auto-fill. Beds/baths/sqft hide under Add property details if you need them.

Assign the photographer

One Photographer picker lists your contractors and internal team together. Pick a contractor and their pay rate + Dropbox upload link come along automatically.

Karen shows up once, not twice — the two lists are now unified.

Create the shoot

Add an optional date, package, and shoot instructions, then Create shoot. You land on the order with the Dropbox upload link already generated.

04 — Hand it off

Send the photographer their link

On any order, the Schedule + team panel is where you assign and dispatch. One tap emails the photographer a branded upload link — no copy-pasting into Gmail.

Email upload link

Assign a contractor, then hit Email upload link to …. They get the address, instructions, and a one-tap Dropbox folder.

Watch for accept / decline

Photographers can accept or hand a shoot back. A decline shows a red banner here and an alert on your Overview so it’s never silent.

Archive when done

Keep the pipeline clean — Archive hides a shoot from the active lists without deleting anything. Reversible anytime.

05 — Deliver

Get the media to the client

Finished work reaches the realtor two ways — a photo gallery link, and the richer Media Room with videos, 360° tours and floor plans.

Generate the gallery link

On the order, Generate delivery link creates a private /gallery page. It tracks views and downloads so you can see when they’ve opened it.

Build the Media Room

Add videos, Matterport/YouTube/Vimeo tours and floor plans. Each item has a publish toggle — nothing shows to the client until you flip it on.

Track it in Deliveries

Deliveries lists every deliverable across jobs — filter by client, jump to the job, copy the link, and see engagement in one place.

06 — Manage people

Clients, photographers & team

Three tidy rosters in the Library group. Edit anyone’s details, set pay, and add new logins.

Clients

Click any agent to edit name, email, phone, brokerage and notes — and see all their properties.

Photographers

Set tiered pay (small / large / 360 add-on), edit contact info, deactivate, and settle weekly pay requests.

Team

Add staff with a role and their weekly availability — they’re schedulable the moment they’re created.

Adding a photographer or team member only needs their name + email — the platform creates their login. They sign in with the same magic-link flow the first time you tell them to.
